Our Philosophy

Why Cretiv?

We believe the best apps are the ones that do their job and get out of the way.

Privacy by Design

No ad SDKs, no marketing trackers, no accounts. The only thing we receive is anonymous crash and performance diagnostics — never your files.

Our apps are free with no ads and no in-app purchases. We build tools we actually want to use ourselves.

User-First

No accounts, no sign-ups, no onboarding walls. Open the app, get your work done.
