Privacy Policy

SS PDF · Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Cretiv ("we") handles information when you use SS PDF.

Information

SS PDF is designed to work on your device. Your documents and the content you create with the app stay on your device and are not uploaded to us.

We may process limited information needed to provide, maintain and improve the app. Information the app saves is kept on your device and protected using industry-standard security measures.

Optional features

Some features, such as opening a file from a link or from a cloud account you choose to connect, use the internet. They access only what they need, and only when you use them. The app asks for access to things like the camera only when a feature you use needs it.

SS PDF's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Some features rely on services from our platform partners, which handle information under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can remove everything the app has stored by clearing its data or uninstalling it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
