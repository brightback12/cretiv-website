Privacy Policy

SS PDF for Android · Last updated 2 September 2026

Your documents never leave your device. SS PDF has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. It does not upload your files, does not send them to us, and does not send them to anyone else. There is no server that receives your data, because there is nothing for it to receive.

What SS PDF stores on your device

All of this lives in the app's own storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Two things are worth calling out plainly:

  • Saved PDF passwords are stored in plain text. They are inside the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device, but they are not encrypted. If that is a concern for a particular document, decline to save its password.
  • Crash reports never leave your device. The app records crashes to a local file. No upload endpoint is configured and no send dialog is shown.

What SS PDF does not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no ad networks.
  • No analytics, telemetry, usage tracking or behavioural profiling.
  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or call data.

Network access

SS PDF only uses the network when you ask it to.

  • Opening a PDF from a link. The app downloads the file from the address you gave it. The request goes to that server and nowhere else.
  • Google Drive (optional). If you choose to connect Google Drive, SS PDF requests read-only access so it can list your files and open the one you pick. It never modifies, deletes or uploads anything, and it never copies your Drive contents anywhere except onto your own device so the file can be displayed. Sign-in is handled by Google; SS PDF never sees your Google password. You can disconnect at any time from inside the app, and revoke access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • OCR language models. Text recognition runs on your device. For some languages the model is delivered by Google Play services, which may download it the first time you use that language. The pages themselves are never uploaded — recognition happens locally.

Files on your device

SS PDF does not request all-files access. It builds your library from:

  • the PDFs your device's media index already knows about, and
  • any folder you explicitly hand over through the Android folder picker.

You can revoke a folder at any time in Android's settings. Individual files you open through the system file picker are read only for as long as you have them open.

Children

SS PDF is a general-purpose document reader. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Decline to save a PDF password.
  • Disconnect Google Drive, or never connect it.
  • Revoke a granted folder in Android's settings.
  • Clear the app's data, or uninstall it, to remove everything described above.
